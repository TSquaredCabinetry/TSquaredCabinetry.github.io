Common questions

Before work begins.

Am I limited in choice of wood or design?

Custom work allows flexibility in wood species, layout, profiles and finish. Cabinet-line selections depend on the manufacturer and series.

Can you help with a whole kitchen or bathroom renovation?

We take on kitchen and bathroom renovation projects, including cabinetry and associated installation work. Scope is confirmed before the project begins.

Can we start with an idea instead of drawings?

Yes. Photos, rough measurements and a description are enough to start the conversation.

Do you serve areas outside Winston-Salem?

Yes. Winston-Salem and Mocksville are the main service areas. We also work in nearby communities; see the full list on the contact page or call to ask about your location.
